ABOUT US

Local guides.
Real coastline.

Dena Tours started small. A couple of guides, a couple of quad bikes, and a stretch of Malindi coastline we'd ridden since we were kids. Word spread, the trips got booked out, and what began as informal rides for visiting friends grew into a full outfit running quad safaris to the Mambrui Dunes and multi day trips into Tsavo. It's still run hands on, and every route is still led by someone who knows the trail personally, not a script.

MISSION

Why we ride

To get visitors off the paved tourist track and onto the real Malindi coast and Tsavo bush. Safe, well guided trips led by people who grew up on these trails, not a postcard version of them.

VISION

Where we're headed

To become the coast's go to local operator for quad and safari adventures. More routes, more trained guides, and a reputation built on trips people are still talking about long after they've flown home.
