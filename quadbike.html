BEFORE YOU RIDE

Safety Information

Every guest gets a helmet, a safety briefing and a supervised practice loop before setting off. Guides ride with the group and set the pace on trail sections. Follow your guide's instructions on speed and spacing at all times, and tell your guide about any medical conditions that could affect riding before you start.
